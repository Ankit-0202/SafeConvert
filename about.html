About SafeConvert

A free, open-source PDF toolkit built with one principle above all else: your files should never leave your device.

Our Mission

SafeConvert was created to solve a problem that millions of people face every day: the need to work with PDF files without sacrificing privacy. Traditional online PDF services require you to upload your documents to remote servers, where they may be stored, analyzed, or even accessed by unauthorized parties. We believe there is a better way.

Our mission is to provide a comprehensive suite of PDF tools that are completely free to use, require no account registration, and process every single file entirely within your web browser. When you use SafeConvert, your documents never leave your computer. There is no server upload, no cloud processing, and no data retention. This is not just a feature; it is the fundamental architecture of our application.

How SafeConvert Works

Unlike traditional online PDF tools that send your files to a server for processing, SafeConvert uses modern web technologies to handle everything locally on your device. Here is a step-by-step explanation of what happens when you use one of our tools:

  1. You select a file. When you drag and drop or choose a PDF from your computer, the file is read directly by your web browser using the File API. It stays in your browser's memory and is never transmitted anywhere.
  2. Processing begins locally. Our tools are powered by JavaScript and WebAssembly (WASM) libraries such as PDF-lib.js, PDF.js, and PDFKit.js. These libraries run entirely within your browser, performing tasks like merging, splitting, compressing, and converting your PDFs at native speeds.
  3. You download the result. Once processing is complete, the output file is generated in your browser and offered as a download. The file goes directly from your browser to your downloads folder, still without ever touching a server.
  4. Memory is cleared. After you download the result or navigate away, the browser releases the memory used during processing. No trace of your document remains.

This client-side approach means SafeConvert works even when you are offline. Once the application has been loaded in your browser, you can disconnect from the internet and continue using most tools without interruption.

Why Privacy Matters for PDF Tools

PDF documents frequently contain some of the most sensitive information people handle: financial statements, medical records, legal contracts, personal identification documents, tax returns, and confidential business reports. When you upload these files to a traditional online PDF service, you are trusting that company with your most private data.

Even services that promise to delete your files after processing still receive and temporarily store them on their servers. This creates multiple points of vulnerability: during the upload, during server-side processing, and during the deletion process. Data breaches at cloud services are unfortunately common, and once your files are uploaded, you lose control over them.

SafeConvert eliminates this risk entirely. Because your files never leave your device, there is nothing for us to breach, nothing to delete, and nothing to mishandle. Your privacy is guaranteed by the architecture itself, not by a promise.

The Technology Behind SafeConvert

SafeConvert is built using a carefully chosen stack of modern, open-source web technologies:

  • PDF-lib.js is the primary library we use for creating and modifying PDF documents. It can merge PDFs, add pages, embed fonts, draw text and images, and much more, all without a server.
  • PDF.js, developed by Mozilla, is used for rendering and reading PDF files in the browser. It powers our PDF viewer and is the same library used by Firefox to display PDFs.
  • WebAssembly (WASM) allows us to run high-performance compiled code directly in your browser. We use WASM modules for intensive tasks like PDF compression and OCR (Optical Character Recognition), achieving speeds comparable to native desktop applications.
  • Vite and TypeScript form the foundation of our build system and codebase, ensuring the application is fast, reliable, and maintainable.
  • Tailwind CSS provides the responsive, modern user interface that adapts to any screen size, from mobile phones to desktop monitors.

What You Can Do with SafeConvert

SafeConvert offers over 100 individual PDF tools organized into several categories. Here is an overview of what you can accomplish:

  • Merge and Split: Combine multiple PDF files into one document, or split a single PDF into separate pages or sections. You can also alternate-merge pages from two documents, extract specific page ranges, and organize pages in any order.
  • Convert: Transform PDFs to and from dozens of formats, including Word (DOCX), Excel, PowerPoint, JPG, PNG, SVG, Markdown, CSV, and many more. You can also convert ebooks (EPUB, MOBI) and office formats (ODT, ODS) to PDF.
  • Edit: Add text, images, watermarks, headers, footers, page numbers, stamps, and signatures to your PDFs. You can also change background colors, invert colors, crop pages, and annotate documents.
  • Compress: Reduce the file size of your PDFs without significant quality loss. Our compression tool uses intelligent algorithms to optimize images, fonts, and document structure.
  • Secure: Encrypt PDFs with passwords, decrypt protected files, change permissions, digitally sign documents, and sanitize sensitive metadata. You can also remove restrictions from PDFs.
  • Organize: Reorder pages, reverse page order, delete unwanted pages, add blank pages, and create booklets. Our organize tool provides a visual drag-and-drop interface.
  • Analyze: View and edit metadata, inspect page dimensions, compare two PDFs side by side, extract tables and images, and validate digital signatures.

Open Source and Community

SafeConvert is open-source software licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPL-3.0). This means the complete source code is publicly available on GitHub. Anyone can inspect the code to verify our privacy claims, report issues, suggest improvements, or contribute new features.

We believe that transparency is essential for trust, especially when it comes to tools that handle sensitive documents. By making our code open source, we invite the global developer community to hold us accountable and help us improve.

Self-Hosting

Because SafeConvert is open source, you can host your own instance on your own infrastructure. We provide detailed deployment guides for popular platforms including Docker, Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, AWS, Nginx, Apache, and Kubernetes. Whether you are an individual who wants full control or an organization with strict data governance requirements, self-hosting gives you complete ownership of the application.

Our Commitment to You

SafeConvert will always be free. There are no premium tiers, no usage limits, no file size restrictions, and no features locked behind a paywall. We sustain the project through minimal, non-intrusive advertising that does not compromise your experience or privacy.

We are committed to continuously improving SafeConvert by adding new tools, improving existing ones, and keeping up with the latest web standards. If you have feedback, feature requests, or want to report a bug, we encourage you to reach out through our Contact page or open an issue on GitHub.
