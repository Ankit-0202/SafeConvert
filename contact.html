How to Get Help

Before reaching out, you may find the answer you are looking for in our existing resources:

  • Frequently Asked Questions — Covers the most common questions about how SafeConvert works, file limits, privacy, browser compatibility, and troubleshooting.
  • About SafeConvert — Explains our mission, the technology behind the tools, and how client-side processing works.
  • Privacy Policy — Details exactly what data we collect (almost none) and how your files are handled.

Bug Reports

If you encounter a problem with one of our tools, we appreciate detailed bug reports. When reporting a bug, please include:

  • The name of the tool you were using (for example, Merge PDF, Compress PDF).
  • Your web browser and version (for example, Chrome 120, Firefox 121, Safari 17).
  • Your operating system (for example, Windows 11, macOS Sonoma, Ubuntu 24).
  • A description of what you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • Any error messages you saw on the screen or in the browser console.

You do not need to share the actual PDF files you were working with. We understand these may contain sensitive information, and our goal is to reproduce the issue without requiring your documents.

Feature Requests

We are always looking for ways to improve SafeConvert. If you have an idea for a new tool or an improvement to an existing one, please let us know through GitHub or email. Community feedback directly shapes our development roadmap.

Response Times

SafeConvert is maintained by a small team of developers who work on this project alongside other responsibilities. We aim to respond to all inquiries within 48 hours on business days. Bug reports filed through GitHub are typically triaged faster as they are visible to the entire contributor community.

Security Issues

If you discover a security vulnerability in SafeConvert, please do not open a public issue on GitHub. Instead, email us directly at support@safeconvert.app with details about the vulnerability. We take security seriously and will work to address any issues promptly. You can also review our Security Policy for more information on responsible disclosure.
