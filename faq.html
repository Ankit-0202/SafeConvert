Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers. Below you will find detailed information about how SafeConvert works, what makes it different from other PDF tools, and how to get the most out of our features.

Privacy and Security

Are my files safe and private?

Absolutely. This is the most important feature of SafeConvert. All processing happens 100% locally in your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to any server, which means we, or anyone else, can never see them. Your privacy is guaranteed by the fundamental architecture of the application, not by a promise.

How is SafeConvert different from other online PDF tools?

Most online PDF services, such as iLovePDF, Smallpdf, or Adobe Acrobat online, require you to upload your files to their servers for processing. Your documents travel over the internet, are processed on remote machines, and are then sent back to you. Even if they promise to delete your files, your data has already been transmitted and temporarily stored.

SafeConvert is fundamentally different. Our tools are powered by JavaScript and WebAssembly libraries that run entirely within your web browser. When you select a file, it is read by your browser's File API and processed using local CPU power. The result is generated directly in your browser's memory and offered as a download. At no point does any data leave your computer. This makes SafeConvert the safest option for handling sensitive documents like financial records, legal contracts, medical files, or personal identification documents.

Can you see the files I process?

No. It is technically impossible for us to see, access, or store your files. Because all processing happens in your browser, your documents never leave your device. We have no server infrastructure designed to receive, process, or store user files. You can verify this by using SafeConvert with your browser's network inspector open. You will see that no file data is transmitted to any external server.

Do you track my activity on SafeConvert?

We care deeply about your privacy. SafeConvert uses Simple Analytics solely to see anonymous visit counts. This means we can know how many users visit our site, but we never know who you are. Simple Analytics does not use cookies, does not track individual users across sessions, and is fully GDPR-compliant. We do not collect personal information, device fingerprints, or browsing history.

Is it safe to process confidential or sensitive documents?

Yes. SafeConvert is specifically designed for this use case. Because your files never leave your device, there is no risk of interception during upload, no risk of data breach on a server, and no risk of unauthorized access. This makes SafeConvert suitable for processing tax returns, medical records, legal agreements, financial statements, HR documents, and any other confidential material. For additional peace of mind, you can disconnect from the internet after the page has loaded and continue using most tools completely offline.

Pricing and Features

Is SafeConvert really free? What is the catch?

Yes, it is completely free, and there is no catch. There are no hidden fees, no subscription plans, no usage limits, and no premium-only features. Every tool is available to every user without restrictions. SafeConvert is an open-source project sustained through minimal, non-intrusive advertising. We believe essential tools for working with PDFs should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their budget.

Are there any file size or usage limitations?

No, we do not impose any artificial limits on file size, the number of files, or how many times you can use a tool. You can merge 100 PDFs, compress a 500 MB document, or convert thousands of pages without hitting any cap. The only practical limitation is the processing power and available memory of your own computer, as very large or complex files may take longer to process or require more RAM. If your browser runs out of memory, try closing other tabs or using a device with more available memory.

Do I need to create an account or sign up?

No. SafeConvert requires no account, no email address, and no personal information of any kind. Simply visit the website and start using any tool immediately. There is nothing to sign up for, no verification steps, and no profile to manage. This also means we have no user database that could be compromised in a data breach.

How many tools does SafeConvert offer?

SafeConvert offers over 100 individual PDF tools. These include tools for merging, splitting, compressing, converting, editing, signing, encrypting, organizing, and analyzing PDF documents. We support conversion between PDFs and dozens of formats including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, JPG, PNG, SVG, Markdown, CSV, EPUB, and many more. You can browse the complete list on our All Tools page.

Technical Details

Do I need an internet connection to use the tools?

After you load the website for the first time, most tools will work completely offline. Because all the processing libraries are loaded into your browser, you can disconnect from the internet and continue to merge, split, compress, and edit your PDFs securely. SafeConvert includes a service worker that caches the application for offline use. Some tools that rely on external resources, such as Markdown to PDF with web-hosted images, or tools requiring WASM modules that have not yet been cached, may need a connection for the initial load.

What technology does SafeConvert use?

SafeConvert is built on modern web technologies. We primarily use JavaScript libraries like PDF-lib.js for creating and modifying PDFs, and PDF.js (developed by Mozilla) for rendering and reading PDF content. For computationally intensive tasks like compression and OCR, we use WebAssembly (WASM) modules that run at near-native speeds in your browser. The application is built with TypeScript, Vite, and Tailwind CSS for a fast, responsive experience. The entire codebase is open source and available on GitHub.

Which web browsers are supported?

SafeConvert works in all modern web browsers that support JavaScript and WebAssembly. This includes Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari, Opera, and Brave. We recommend using the latest version of your preferred browser for the best performance and compatibility. Some older browsers or mobile browsers with limited WebAssembly support may experience reduced functionality with certain advanced tools.

Can I use SafeConvert on my phone or tablet?

Yes. SafeConvert is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets running iOS or Android. You can use it directly in your mobile browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox). However, keep in mind that processing very large PDF files on mobile devices may be slower due to limited CPU power and available memory compared to a desktop or laptop computer. For large batch operations, we recommend using a desktop browser.

Can I install SafeConvert as a desktop app?

Yes. SafeConvert is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it on your device directly from your browser. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar or go to the browser menu and select "Install SafeConvert." On mobile, use your browser's "Add to Home Screen" option. Once installed, SafeConvert will appear in your app launcher and can be opened without navigating to the website. The installed app also works offline.

Troubleshooting

Why did my PDF fail to process?

Failures are rare but can happen for a few reasons:

  • The PDF might be corrupted or not compliant with the PDF specification.
  • The file could be encrypted with a password you do not have. Please use our Decrypt tool first.
  • The file might be a special "XFA" or dynamic form-based PDF, which some of our tools cannot process. XFA is a proprietary format created by Adobe that is not widely supported outside of Adobe products.
  • Your browser may have run out of memory while processing a very large file. Try closing other tabs or using a device with more RAM.
  • The PDF may contain unusual embedded fonts or resources that cause issues with specific tools. Try a different tool or simplify the document first.

The page is loading slowly. What can I do?

SafeConvert loads several JavaScript and WebAssembly libraries to enable client-side processing. On slower internet connections, the initial page load may take a moment. Here are some tips to improve performance:

  • Make sure you are using a modern, up-to-date browser.
  • Close unnecessary browser tabs to free up memory.
  • After the first visit, the application is cached by a service worker, so subsequent visits will load much faster.
  • If you installed SafeConvert as a PWA, the app loads from cache automatically.

My converted PDF looks different from the original. Why?

PDF conversion, especially between formats like PDF to Word or PDF to Excel, is inherently imperfect. PDFs are designed as a final-format document with precise visual layout, while formats like DOCX and XLSX are designed for editing with flexible layouts. Some visual differences are expected, particularly with complex layouts, embedded fonts, or non-standard formatting. For the best results, we recommend:

  • Using simpler source documents with standard fonts.
  • Reviewing the output and making manual adjustments as needed.
  • For image-based PDFs (scanned documents), try using the OCR tool first to extract text before converting.

Where are my processed files saved?

When processing is complete, your browser will prompt you to download the result file. The file is saved to your default downloads folder, or you may be asked to choose a location depending on your browser settings. The processed file is not stored on any server or in the cloud. If you close the browser tab before downloading, the processed file is lost and you will need to process the original file again.

Open Source and Self-Hosting

Is SafeConvert open source?

Yes. SafeConvert is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3 (AGPL-3.0), which means the complete source code is publicly available on GitHub. Anyone can inspect the code to verify our privacy claims, contribute new features, report bugs, or fork the project. We believe transparency is essential for trust.

Can I host SafeConvert on my own server?

Yes. We provide detailed deployment guides for hosting SafeConvert on your own infrastructure. Supported platforms include Docker, Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, AWS, Nginx, Apache, and Kubernetes. Self-hosting is ideal for organizations with strict data governance requirements or for individuals who want complete control over their tools. Visit our GitHub repository for deployment instructions.
